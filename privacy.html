Privacy Policy

This application is a private, single-user Home Assistant installation operated by its owner for personal home automation.

Data accessed

The application accesses only the owner's own Google account data (such as Nest devices and Google Calendar) through Google's OAuth APIs, and only to display and control that data within the owner's home automation system.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's private home server. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Users

The application has no users other than its owner and is not offered to the public.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings under Security, Third-party apps with account access.
